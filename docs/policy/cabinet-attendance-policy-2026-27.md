# Cabinet attendance policy and point system, 2026–27

Transcribed from the two source documents E-Board sent over (received 2026-09-24), so the rules live next to the code. The originals are **AMENDED HSA CABINET ATTENDANCE POLICY 2026-2027** (the contract Cabinet signs) and the **HSA Point Systems 2026-2027** sheet. Where these differ from `CONTEXT.md`, see [Where the sources disagree](#where-the-sources-disagree); `CONTEXT.md` stays canonical for the code.

## Attendance policy (the signed contract)

In effect from September 20, 2026 (the document calls it "Monday", but that date is a Sunday). Applies to E-Board and Cabinet.

**Required events** (glossary: Core Events)
1. HSA Cabinet Thursdays
2. HSA GBMs
3. Cabinet Retreats
4. Cabinet Orientation
5. Hispanic-Latine Student Assembly
6. One Hispanic-Latine Heritage Month event

**Required once per semester** (glossary: Semester Requirements)
1. OPA General Events
2. OPA Solidarity Sessions
3. HSA Operations Events
4. HSA Programming Events
5. HSA Fundraising Events
6. HSA Service Events
7. Affiliate Organization Events (MLP Directors are exempt)
8. Internal HSA Socials
9. Cabinet-Specific Socials
10. Tabling (1 hour)

**Optional events** (glossary: Additional Events). Proof of attendance must be sent through the "Additional Event Form".
1. Additional Tabling
2. Additional MLP Events
3. Additional HSA Fundraising Events
4. Additional OPA/Operations/Programming Events
5. Other Affiliated Organization events
6. Other CRASH Events

**Points and standing**
- Every event above is worth 1 Cabinet Point, except Cabinet Thursdays and the Internal HSA / Cabinet-Specific socials.
- 20 Cabinet Points must be earned by Cabinet Graduation to stay in good standing.
- Missing a required event without a valid excuse earns an **infraction** (glossary: Strike). 3 infractions lead to **probation**: a meeting between the Cabinet Member and E-Board to agree a point make-up plan.
- Completion of Cabinet is not awarded to anyone still on probation, or short of 20 Cabinet Points, at Cabinet Graduation.

**Excuses**
- Valid excuses (E-Board reviews others case by case): class (including Study Edge, exam reviews, tutoring); exams (day of); work; events or meetings of orgs that charge dues (e.g. Greek orgs); severe health conditions, including mental health; doctors' appointments; out of town / family in town; school or departmental organization meetings (e.g. Warrington).
- An excuse only counts if it reaches the HSA Secretary through the "Valid Excuse Form" **before** the event; otherwise the miss earns an infraction.
- Excessive use of valid excuses may be reviewed case by case.

**Changes** to the policy are decided and announced by E-Board with two weeks' notice.

## Where the sources disagree

Raised for E-Board; nothing here changes `CONTEXT.md` until they answer.

1. **MLP Spring VE threshold:** the Cabinet System tab says 10, the Voter Eligibility tab says 8. The glossary uses 8.
2. **HLHM frequency:** the policy says one HLHM event (for the year); the sheet says once per semester. The glossary uses one per year.
3. **Probation gates graduation:** the policy withholds Cabinet completion from anyone still on probation. The glossary says Core Events and Semester Requirements "do not gate" Graduating Cabinet; 3+ open Strikes (probation) may need to.
4. **Socials:** the policy's two socials are *Internal HSA* and *Cabinet-Specific*; the sheet's notes define three (External, Internal, Cabinet-specific). The glossary's pair is Internal + External, which doesn't line up with the policy's names.
5. **"Max" 20 Cabinet Points:** the sheet says max, the policy says 20 must be earned. The glossary treats 20 as the target, not a cap.
6. **Excuse deadline:** the policy requires the excuse *before* the event; the glossary has no deadline rule for an Excused Absence.
